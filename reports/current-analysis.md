# Current observatory analysis

## Available evidence

Validated metric rows: 162,130.

- Baseline: 3,595,690 requests; 30-day closure rate 91.0%.
- Study: 3,963,901 requests; 30-day closure rate 90.4%.

## Operational screening

50 comparable agency-service-neighborhood combinations are retained, requiring at least 30 mature requests in both periods. The JSON file preserves the top 50.

## Limits

- 311 reports measure reported demand, not all public service need or unique incidents.
- Closure is an administrative event and does not prove that a problem was resolved.
- Priority rows are descriptive screening flags, not causal findings or agency rankings.
- Socioeconomic context is not yet available.
- The policy evidence layer is pending. Public government sources such as NYC
  Legistar and the NYC Mayor's Management Report are the reference points for that layer.
