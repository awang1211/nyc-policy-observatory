# Data status

| Dataset | Intended use | Status | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| NYC 311, October 2024–September 2026 | RQ2 demand, service performance and operational screening | Complete and validated | Analyze now |
| Community district and NTA geography | Neighborhood labels and boundaries | Official assets identified | Integrate into dashboard |
| ACS population, poverty and income | Socioeconomic context and denominators | Not loaded | Record as a material gap; do not infer equity from 311 alone |
| Council bills and local laws | RQ1 policy coverage | Official Open Data ends February 2025 | Not usable for the study year; seek another official release without delaying RQ2 |
| Mayor and agency policy publications | RQ1 policy coverage and implementation evidence | Automated sites block hosted retrieval | Preserve as an access gap; do not claim complete registry |
| Agency performance indicators | Cross-check operational performance | Current candidate assets under review | Load only if 2025–2026 fields are verified |

The project proceeds with a complete descriptive RQ2 analysis from validated 311
records. RQ1 remains incomplete until study-period policy evidence can be sourced
and reviewed. Missing sources are never replaced with estimates, inferred policy
status, or unsupported causal claims.
