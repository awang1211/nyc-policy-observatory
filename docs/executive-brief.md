Study: October 2025–September 2026. Baseline: October 2024–September 2025. Prepared 27 October 2026.

### Verified findings are not yet available
Neither research question can yet be answered from verified observations in this delivery. Network policy blocked the requested Cube workspace and official NYC/Census endpoints. ### What the local project prepares
A reproducible pipeline connects archived official evidence, a relational database, reviewed policy records, validated metric calculations and six Cube semantic model files to three interactive local pages. These are software capabilities, not research findings or deployed Cube Cloud objects.

### RQ1 — Policy responsiveness and implementation
Build a broad inventory across municipal policy domains using Council actions, Mayor records, budgets and agency delivery evidence. Deduplicate these records into canonical initiatives. Keep announced, enacted and implementation-verified stages distinct. Assess reviewed topical alignment with service needs without attributing later metric changes to a policy.

### RQ2 — Neighborhood equity and operational priorities
Compare the same agency and service across community districts using request counts and matched 30-day closure cohorts. Keep borough ACS context at borough geography. Retain unknown locations, missing denominators and immature requests. Administrative closure does not prove service resolution.

# Decision use & release conditions
## From a local foundation to a source-backed observatory

### An investigation queue, not an automatic judgment
The default rule requires at least 30 eligible requests in each period and a closure-rate decline of at least 10 percentage points. These adjustable descriptive thresholds do not establish statistical significance or agency failure. No populated queue is available yet.

### Controls that protect comparisons
Both periods are seasonally matched. Late September requests lacking 30 days of follow-up and corresponding baseline requests are excluded from closure-rate denominators, while remaining in volume counts. Rates use summed numerators and denominators. Cell medians and p90s are not additive. Fiscal-year indicators retain their actual periods.

### Evidence and access required
The source plan includes NYC Open Data 311, Council Legistar and enacted laws, Mayor/agency publications, MMR and 2024 ACS five-year county estimates. Exact quotations, event dates, retrieval timestamps and source hashes preserve lineage. These are acquisition targets; official retrieval and policy review remain outstanding.

### Next actions
Activate the saved domain changes through environment settings and publication. Establish authorized Cube access. Inspect actual connections, models, Workbooks and Dashboard features. Acquire and validate records, review policy breadth and deduplication, publish to an authorized database, reconcile Cube queries, and author native pages through supported features. No Cube objects or GitHub push have been performed.

### Limits on interpretation
Reporting propensity, repeated complaints, service mix, mutable records, closure practices, policy coverage and geography/fiscal mismatches limit interpretation. No community-district poverty allocation, causal estimate or significance-adjusted ranking is supplied. Release substantive conclusions only after actual-source reconciliation and evidence review.
